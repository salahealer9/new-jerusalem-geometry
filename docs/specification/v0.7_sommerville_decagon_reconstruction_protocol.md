# v0.7 — Sommerville decagon reconstruction and constraint-audit protocol

## Status

Preregistered before implementation of the v0.7 decagon geometry.

## Research question

Can the Sommerville decagon associated with Michell's New Jerusalem geometry
be reconstructed from explicit geometric constraints without using its known
historical dimensional values as tuning targets?

The audit asks:

1. what decagon-related construction information is actually stated or drawn
   in the permitted primary-source corpus;
2. whether that information determines a unique decagon geometry;
3. how a source-supported construction differs from a regular-decagon
   baseline;
4. what geometric side classes and ratios follow before dimensional scaling;
5. only after the normalized geometry is frozen, how those results compare
   retrospectively with the registered historical dimensional claims.

## Source boundary

The baseline source corpus remains:

- John Michell, *City of Revelation*;
- John Michell with Allan Brown, *How the World Is Made*.

External attribution verification remains outside the v0.7 baseline unless
introduced later as a separately documented provenance exercise.

## Relationship to earlier checkpoints

v0.7 inherits the frozen v0.5 `NJG_MICHELL` geometry and the evidential
discipline established in v0.6.

The v0.5 geometry is not to be modified merely to improve decagon agreement.

The v0.6 historical-metrology audit established that the known Sommerville
decagon quantities are already exposed historical information.

Accordingly, they cannot become blind or independent predictions in v0.7.

## Historical-target firewall

The following known historical quantities are development-exposed targets:

```text
6336
6300
176:175
1/5 of 31680
````

They must not be used to:

* choose geometric parameters;
* choose among candidate constructions;
* rotate or phase a candidate;
* set a radius, apothem, scale, or offset;
* select vertex correspondences;
* choose a side-class partition;
* define an optimisation objective;
* define an acceptance tolerance;
* reject an otherwise source-supported candidate because its dimensions are
  numerically less close;
* alter the inherited v0.5 geometry.

Before the normalized decagon geometry is frozen, these target values must not
appear in geometric construction modules or geometric SVG generators.

Their allowed role is downstream retrospective comparison after the geometric
freeze.

## No-refit rule

Once a candidate construction is frozen for dimensional evaluation:

* no continuous parameter may be changed;
* no discrete candidate may be switched because of the historical dimensions;
* no alternative scaling convention may be selected because it improves
  historical agreement;
* no new tolerance may be introduced after residuals are seen.

Any later geometric amendment requires a new explicitly versioned protocol.

## Phase structure

### Phase 7A — source extraction and claim registry

Extract all decagon-specific statements and source-figure constraints from the
permitted primary texts.

Create a structured registry distinguishing:

```text
source_stated
source_drawn
source_attributed
project_inference
```

The extraction must distinguish geometric information from dimensional
information.

Historical dimensional values are registered for provenance but remain behind
the target firewall.

### Phase 7B — candidate construction families

Define the geometric candidate set before dimensional comparison.

At minimum it must contain:

```text
REGULAR_DECAGON_BASELINE
```

This is a deliberately simple control model.

Additional candidates may be admitted only when motivated by Phase 7A source
geometry.

Each candidate must specify:

* inherited construction objects used;
* free geometric parameters, if any;
* discrete choices, if any;
* construction sequence;
* expected symmetries;
* side-class definition;
* source basis;
* whether the candidate is source-supported or project-inferred.

Candidate families must not be generated by searching for agreement with the
historical dimensional targets.

### Phase 7C — normalized geometric reconstruction

Implement the candidates entirely in normalized geometric coordinates.

Primary outputs include vertices, edges, side lengths, side classes, angles,
radii/apothems where relevant, and construction dependencies.

No historical dimensional scale is applied in this phase.

### Phase 7D — constraint and degrees-of-freedom audit

For each surviving candidate report:

* number of inherited fixed quantities;
* number of newly introduced continuous parameters;
* number of discrete choices;
* which constraints determine each quantity;
* whether the construction is unique under those constraints;
* whether multiple candidates remain geometrically admissible.

The audit must distinguish:

```text
fully_determined
discretely_underdetermined
continuously_underdetermined
```

A candidate with an unconstrained parameter must not be described as a
prediction of the historical dimensions.

### Phase 7E — geometric freeze and dimensional reconstruction

Freeze the selected normalized geometric candidate before applying historical
scale.

The frozen geometric artifact must receive a deterministic hash.

Only after this freeze may the already registered dimensional scale be applied.

The dimensional transformation must reuse the frozen metrological framework
from v0.6 unless a prospectively documented source reason requires otherwise.

### Phase 7F — retrospective historical comparison

Compare the frozen dimensional reconstruction with the exposed historical
decagon claims.

These comparisons are retrospective.

They are not:

* blind predictions;
* independent validations;
* p-values;
* statistical significance tests.

Residual formulas must be preregistered before calculation.

No aggregate goodness-of-fit statistic is required.

### Phase 7G — checkpoint closeout

Produce the v0.7 synthesis, release-readiness audit, final deterministic hashes,
signed merge, and signed annotated `v0.7.0` checkpoint tag.

No GitHub Release or Zenodo record is implied by the checkpoint.

## SVG plan

v0.7 will maintain distinct visual outputs for distinct evidential roles.

### 1. `sommerville_decagon_source_constraints.svg`

Purpose:

Visualize only the inherited New Jerusalem geometry and decagon-relevant source
constraints extracted in Phase 7A.

It must not display historical dimensional target values.

It must not imply a preferred reconstruction where the source does not yet
determine one.

### 2. `sommerville_decagon_regular_baseline.svg`

Purpose:

Show the regular-decagon control construction on the same normalized geometric
framework.

Its role is methodological:

> determine what follows from ordinary regular-decagon geometry before
> attributing any special effect to a Sommerville-specific construction.

It must not be tuned against historical dimensions.

### 3. `sommerville_decagon_reconstruction.svg`

Purpose:

Show the frozen preferred source-supported reconstruction, if Phase 7A–7D
justify one.

This is the canonical geometric SVG for the preferred v0.7 candidate.

It must be generated without dimensional target input.

### 4. `sommerville_decagon_comparison.svg`

Purpose:

Compare the regular baseline with the frozen source-supported reconstruction.

It may display:

* vertex displacement;
* angular differences;
* normalized side classes;
* normalized side ratios;
* construction constraints.

It must not use historical dimensional target values as candidate-selection
criteria.

### 5. `sommerville_decagon_dimensional_audit.svg`

Status:

Downstream / optional.

Purpose:

After the geometric freeze, display the frozen dimensional reconstruction
beside the registered historical values.

This SVG is explicitly a retrospective comparison artifact.

It must be generated only after the Phase 7E geometry hash is frozen.

## SVG reproducibility requirements

All generated SVGs must:

* use transparent backgrounds;
* use normalized coordinates for geometric construction;
* be deterministic under repeated generation;
* expose semantic object IDs where practical;
* preserve construction-role labels;
* distinguish inherited geometry from newly introduced decagon geometry;
* avoid embedding machine-specific absolute paths;
* be reproducible from committed scripts and frozen inputs.

Where practical, rendered objects should map to machine-readable provenance
records in the same spirit as the v0.5 `NJG_MICHELL` provenance architecture.

## Regular baseline role

The regular-decagon baseline is not a straw man.

It is required to determine whether the historical unequal-side structure
reflects:

1. an effect already inherent in the inherited New Jerusalem framework;
2. only a minor perturbation of a regular decagon;
3. or a materially different source-specific construction.

This comparison must be made geometrically before dimensional residuals are
introduced.

## Candidate-selection rule

Candidate preference before Phase 7E may use only preregistered geometric and
source-consistency criteria.

Permitted examples include:

* satisfaction of explicit source incidences;
* satisfaction of explicit source tangencies;
* agreement with stated construction dependencies;
* geometric determinacy;
* symmetry relations;
* source-plate geometry where formally registered.

Historical dimensional agreement is forbidden as a candidate-selection
criterion.

## Source-plate measurements

If a printed decagon figure is sufficiently precise to digitize, any
digitisation protocol must be frozen before quantitative plate fitting.

Plate measurements must be kept separate from historical dimensional targets.

If the plate is too imprecise to discriminate candidate constructions, that
must be reported rather than compensated for by fitting the known dimensions.

## Interpretation boundary

v0.7 is a geometric and historical-constraint audit.

It does not establish:

* that ancient builders used the reconstructed decagon;
* that the dimensional correspondences have symbolic significance;
* that the construction demonstrates a physical law;
* that exposed historical targets are independent predictions.

Symbolic, harmonic, or interpretive analysis remains downstream of the frozen
geometric result.

## Stopping rule

The v0.7 reconstruction phase may terminate with any of the following legitimate
outcomes:

```text
UNIQUE_SOURCE_SUPPORTED_RECONSTRUCTION
MULTIPLE_SOURCE_SUPPORTED_RECONSTRUCTIONS
SOURCE_GEOMETRY_UNDERDETERMINED
SOURCE_PLATE_INSUFFICIENT
NO_SOURCE_SUPPORTED_DECAGON_RECONSTRUCTION
```

A negative or underdetermined result is not grounds for tuning against the
historical dimensional targets.

## Preregistered scientific priority

The principal v0.7 result is the construction audit itself.

Numerical closeness to the already known historical dimensions is secondary
retrospective evidence and must remain labelled as such.
