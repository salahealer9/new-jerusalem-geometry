## Displacement definitions

Let:

```text
Delta_theta
```

be the smallest signed angular difference between an observed direction and
its fixed exact direction.

Then:

```text
angular residual degrees
    = degrees(Delta_theta)

arc displacement
    = 7*|Delta_theta|

chord displacement
    = 14*sin(|Delta_theta|/2)
```

with `Delta_theta` in radians.

For the plate endpoint `(x_i,y_i)` and exact vertex `(X_i,Y_i)`:

```text
radial residual
    = sqrt(x_i^2+y_i^2) - 7

direct Euclidean displacement
    = sqrt((x_i-X_i)^2 + (y_i-Y_i)^2)
```
