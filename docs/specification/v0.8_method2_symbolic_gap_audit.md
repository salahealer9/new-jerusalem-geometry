```text

```

The exact regular gaps for the propagated sets are:


## Mechanical signature derivation

For each frozen sorted point, reconstruct its unnormalized semantic angle:




For adjacent sorted points, including the cyclic wrap, derive:

