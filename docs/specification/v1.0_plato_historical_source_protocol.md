# v1.0 Plato / Michell historical-source audit protocol

## Status

`PREREGISTERED_BEFORE_SOURCE_EXTRACTION`

This protocol governs **v1.0.0 — Plato and the Historical Cosmological Context**.
It is frozen before v1.0 source extraction, numerical reconstruction, or
historical synthesis.

The branch starts from signed release `v0.9.0`, peeled commit:

```text
8c5ec9e9c1b07864f6d0a35e3b278f69f8023e64
```

The package remains `0.9.0` during development and changes to `1.0.0` only at
release closeout.

## Research questions

### Q1 — Plato literal content

What does Plato's *Republic*, in the Myth of Er, explicitly state about the
spindle and its nested whorls, including ordering and relative widths?

No Michell-derived numerical widths may be imported into the Plato layer.

### Q2 — Michell explicit content

What numerical sequence, ordering rule, central-radius rule, and scale factor
does Michell explicitly state or clearly use in his Plato/whorl construction?

No project reconstruction may be promoted to a Michell statement without
direct source support.

### Q3 — Deterministic reconstruction

Can the ordered widths and cumulative radii be reproduced using only frozen,
source-supported inputs?

No permutation search, free scale fitting, least-squares fitting, post-hoc
sequence substitution, or target-driven selection is allowed.

### Q4 — Dimensional correspondences

Which reconstructed radii or widths correspond numerically to already-frozen
New Jerusalem dimensions, and which correspondences are explicit in Michell
rather than project-derived?

### Q5 — Historical boundary

What historical conclusion is warranted by the surviving source chain?
Numerical agreement alone cannot establish Plato's intention, ancient
transmission, hidden authorship, or metaphysical truth.

## Provenance classes

Every v1.0 claim must use exactly one class:

- `DIRECT_PLATO_TEXT`
- `MICHELL_EXPLICIT`
- `PROJECT_RECONSTRUCTION`
- `SUPPORTING_HISTORICAL_CONTEXT`
- `ARCHIVAL_PENDING`
- `INTERPRETIVE_EXCLUDED`

A later numerical assignment cannot be back-projected into Plato.

## Source hierarchy

The canonical source inventory is:

```text
docs/sources/v1.0_plato_source_manifest.csv
```

Priority order:

1. Plato primary text for the Myth of Er.
2. John Michell, *The Dimensions of Paradise*.
3. Existing Michell project sources, including *City of Revelation* and
   *How the World Is Made*, only where relevant as supporting cross-checks.
4. Roberts-edited Glastonbury material as contextual evidence only.
5. Sommerville material remains `ARCHIVAL_PENDING` unless primary archival
   material is obtained and frozen.

No source may silently move upward in this hierarchy.

## Source-freezing rule

Before a source can support a v1.0 claim, the exact edition, scan,
transcription, or stable digital witness used must be recorded.

A web-search result, bookseller description, secondary quotation, or model
memory is not a frozen historical source.

## Plato-layer rules

The Plato extraction must preserve what the frozen primary text actually
states and separately record:

- number of whorls;
- stated width order;
- other stated ordinal properties;
- explicit numerical values, if any;
- absence of explicit numerical values, if applicable.

Ordinal information remains ordinal until a later source explicitly assigns
numbers to it.

## Michell-layer rules

The Michell extraction must distinguish:

- numbers Michell explicitly prints;
- sequences he explicitly names or displays;
- orderings he explicitly assigns;
- calculations directly implied by his printed construction;
- calculations first performed by this project.

If the required Michell source cannot be frozen, the corresponding claim stays
unresolved.

## Reconstruction rules

Permitted only after the textual layers are frozen:

- deterministic reordering by an explicit source rule;
- exact or rational arithmetic;
- cumulative sums;
- an explicitly sourced central-radius rule;
- an explicitly sourced scale factor;
- comparison with frozen New Jerusalem dimensions.

Prohibited:

- permutation search;
- nearest-match selection;
- free scale fitting;
- least-squares fitting;
- post-hoc sequence substitution;
- choosing a musical sequence because it fits the target;
- importing the v0.9 exact-heptagon/trisection result into the historical
  argument;
- treating numerical agreement as proof of transmission.

## Interpretive exclusion boundary

The v1.0 historical layer excludes gematria as evidential proof, metaphysical
conclusions, later interpretive frameworks, and claims of ancient transmission
unless primary evidence directly establishes them.

- **10C** — literal Plato extraction with no Michell numerical leakage.
- **10D** — literal Michell extraction with no project reconstruction promoted

## Stop conditions

Stop rather than infer through a gap if a required source cannot be frozen, a
translation ambiguity affects the numerical chain, a parameter lacks a source
or preregistered project role, or a conflict could only be resolved after
seeing the target.

## Non-claims

This protocol does not establish that Plato knew the New Jerusalem diagram,
that Michell's interpretation is ancient, that Sommerville contributed to the
Platonic/sevenfold construction, or that numerical correspondence proves
historical transmission.
