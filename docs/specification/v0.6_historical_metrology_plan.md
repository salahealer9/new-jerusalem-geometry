# v0.6.0 — Historical metrology and prediction-status audit

## Status

Planned feature milestone.

The immutable geometric baseline is the signed `v0.5.0` checkpoint.

No v0.6 analysis may refit, rotate, rescale, replace, or otherwise modify the
frozen `NJG_MICHELL` geometry in order to improve agreement with a historical
dimensional or metrological claim.

## Central question

Given the geometry frozen at `v0.5.0`, which dimensional and metrological
quantities stated or reported in John Michell's primary texts are:

1. direct construction inputs or unit definitions;
2. exact or conventional consequences of those inputs;
3. historical numerical targets already involved in model development;
4. historical numerical targets not previously used to select the relevant
   geometric feature;
5. not evaluable from the present `v0.5.0` geometry?

For every evaluable quantity, what numerical value follows from the frozen
geometry under the source-stated unit convention, and what is its residual
from the historical value?

## Primary-source scope

The baseline v0.6 source corpus is restricted to:

- John Michell, *City of Revelation*;
- John Michell with Allan Brown, *How the World Is Made*.

These books are authoritative for what Michell states, adopts, or reports.

They are not automatically treated as independent historical verification of
the older metrological systems or measurements that Michell attributes to
other researchers.

Direct verification of an external attribution may be added later as a
separate source-verification layer, without changing the frozen primary-source
audit.

## Attribution taxonomy

Each metrological record must carry one attribution class:

- `michell_stated`
  - Michell directly states the numerical or metrological relation in his own
    exposition.

- `michell_adopts_external`
  - Michell adopts a unit, measurement, or relation that he attributes to an
    earlier external source or researcher.

- `michell_reports_other`
  - Michell reports an analysis or dimensional result explicitly attributed
    to another named researcher.

- `project_derived`
  - The present project computes a value from the frozen geometry.

No `project_derived` value may be entered as though it were a historical
source claim.

## Prediction-status taxonomy

At least one of the following statuses must be assigned to every evaluated
claim:

- `construction_input`
  - The historical number directly parameterizes the geometry or its scale.
    Agreement is not a prediction.

- `unit_definition`
  - The value defines a unit conversion. Agreement after applying the
    conversion is not a prediction.

- `construction_identity`
  - The quantity follows algebraically from source-imposed inputs or from the
    source's explicit conventional arithmetic.

- `development_used_target`
  - The historical quantity was available during development or selection of
    the relevant geometric model. Agreement is retrospective and must not be
    described as independent prediction.

- `frozen_unused_target`
  - The historical target was not used to choose, fit, or calibrate the
    relevant frozen geometric feature. This is the strongest available
    forward-prediction category inside v0.6.

- `descriptive_correspondence`
  - A numerical relationship is recorded but is not being tested as a
    generative prediction.

- `not_evaluable_from_v0_5`
  - The required geometric object or quantity is absent from the frozen
    v0.5.0 reconstruction.

The audit must err toward the weaker category whenever provenance is
ambiguous.

## Source families to inventory

### A. Earth–Moon / squared-circle dimensions

Source quantities include the Earth and Moon dimensions used by Michell,
their combined-radius construction, and the `31680` perimeter/circumference
arithmetic under the source's `pi = 22/7` convention.

These values substantially define the normalized core and must therefore be
classified primarily as construction inputs or construction identities, not
as new v0.6 predictions.

### B. New Jerusalem Figure 12 wall dimensions

The source inventory must include the earlier *City of Revelation* statements
that:

- the average wall side is very close to `3264 ft` or `1200 MY`;
- the resulting twelve-side perimeter is `14400 MY`;
- the area is about `120,000,000 square ft`.

The provenance audit must determine which of these quantities were previously
used during development or assessment of the wall reconstruction.

### C. Later irregular-dodecagon dimensions

The source inventory must separately record the later analysis reported in
*How the World Is Made*:

- four longer polar sides near `3280 ft`;
- eight shorter sides near `3270 ft`;
- enclosed area near `120,000,000 square ft`;
- working tolerance `1:2500`;
- perimeter virtually `36000` old-English feet;
- old-English foot related `12:11` to the current foot.

Michell explicitly attributes this dimensional analysis to Ian Sommerville.
The attribution must be preserved.

Because these values were already consulted during development of the
preferred polar-pivot wall, they are presumptively
`development_used_target`, not independent predictions.

### D. Metrological unit definitions and adopted values

The registry must include source-stated or source-adopted values relevant to
the New Jerusalem analysis, including where applicable:

- megalithic yard: `2.72 ft`;
- Sumerian foot: `13.2 in = 1.1 English ft`;
- stadion/furlong relation in Michell's New Jerusalem treatment;
- New Jerusalem cubit: `1.728 ft`;
- old-English-foot/current-foot ratio `12:11`.

Every unit must retain its source attribution and must not be silently
substituted by a modern or alternative value.

### E. Additional dimensional claims

Additional Michell-stated quantities may be registered if they can be mapped
to an explicit v0.5 geometric object.

A claim with no corresponding frozen object remains
`not_evaluable_from_v0_5`; v0.6 must not extend the geometry merely to make
that claim testable.

## Numerical conventions

### Frozen geometry

All geometric coordinates and topology come from the signed `v0.5.0`
checkpoint.

No source-plate coordinates, fitted registration transforms, metrological
target values, or downstream residuals may alter the predictor.

### Scale

Scale conversions must be explicit.

Where a historical dimensional number is itself one of the inputs that fixes
the scale, subsequent agreement produced merely by that scale must be marked
as input-dependent.

### Pi

The audit must keep distinct:

- Euclidean `pi`;
- Michell's explicit conventional `22/7` arithmetic.

A value exact only under `22/7` must not be reported as Euclidean exact.

### Residuals

For each evaluable target the default report must include:

- frozen predicted value;
- historical/source value;
- signed residual;
- absolute residual;
- relative residual;
- percent residual;
- prediction-status class;
- attribution class.

Approximate historical targets must remain labelled approximate.

## No-refit rule

After this plan is committed:

- no geometric parameter may be optimized against a metrological target;
- no wall candidate may be switched because another candidate gives a closer
  historical number;
- no unit conversion may be tuned;
- no tolerance may be chosen after seeing a residual;
- no failed target may be removed from the registered inventory because it
  weakens the overall numerical agreement.

Any genuinely new geometric hypothesis belongs in a later version and must
not be introduced inside the v0.6 metrology audit.

## Phase structure

### Phase 6A — Source/metrology registry

Create a machine-readable inventory of primary-source unit definitions,
historical dimensional claims, attributions, source pages, figures, and
prediction-status classifications.

No prediction computation is performed before this registry is frozen.

### Phase 6B — Formal unit system

Implement exact or explicitly approximate unit conversions from the
registered source definitions.

The implementation must preserve source convention and attribution.

### Phase 6C — Frozen geometric predictions

Map registered evaluable quantities onto the `v0.5.0` `NJG_MICHELL`
geometry and compute their dimensional values without refitting.

### Phase 6D — Prediction-status / leakage audit

For each target, determine whether the relevant historical number was:

- an input;
- an algebraic identity;
- previously used in model development;
- unused by the relevant frozen construction;
- not evaluable.

This classification is part of the scientific result, not merely metadata.

### Phase 6E — Residual and sensitivity report

Produce deterministic residual tables.

Sensitivity checks may compare explicitly registered source conventions, such
as Euclidean `pi` versus the historical `22/7` convention, but must not turn
those alternatives into free fit parameters.

### Phase 6F — Optional external-attribution verification

Where feasible, directly inspect external works that Michell cites or reports
(for example an original metrological or dimensional source).

This layer verifies attribution and historical provenance only.

It may not change the primary-source numerical registry or the frozen
geometric predictor.

### Phase 6G — v0.6.0 checkpoint

Release readiness requires:

- complete source registry;
- deterministic unit conversions;
- deterministic frozen predictions;
- explicit prediction-status classification for every registered target;
- no unregistered target deletion;
- no refitting;
- full tests passing;
- clean deterministic outputs;
- a checkpoint document separating genuine prediction from input,
  identity, retrospective agreement, and descriptive correspondence.

## Expected scientific outcome

The audit is not preregistered to show that Michell's metrology is correct.

The expected useful outcome is a provenance-resolved partition of the
numerical correspondences:

- which are built into the construction;
- which are conventional identities;
- which are retrospective because they influenced model development;
- which, if any, survive as genuine frozen unused targets;
- which cannot presently be tested.

A numerically close match is scientifically stronger only when its provenance
supports the stronger prediction category.

## Version boundary

The package version remains `0.5.0` throughout development.

It advances to `0.6.0` only after the registered v0.6 audit is complete and
the release-readiness gate passes.
