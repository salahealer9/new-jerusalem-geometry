# v0.6 Phase 6D — prediction-status and leakage audit protocol

## Status

Preregistered evidential-classification protocol.

Phase 6D is downstream of the frozen Phase 6C dimensional predictions and
upstream of every v0.6 historical residual calculation.

Its purpose is to prevent numerical agreement from changing the evidential
status assigned to a historical quantity.

## Central question

For each registered historical quantity, which evidential category applies
before looking at any v0.6 residual?

The answer must come from source role and project provenance, not numerical
closeness.

## Frozen prediction-status taxonomy

The Phase 6A taxonomy remains:

```text
construction_input
unit_definition
construction_identity
development_used_target
frozen_unused_target
descriptive_correspondence
not_evaluable_from_v0_5
```

Phase 6D does not silently rewrite the Phase 6A registry.

Instead it emits an audit record containing:

- the frozen Phase 6A status;
- the Phase 6D audit verdict;
- the reason for that verdict;
- any pre-v0.5 development evidence;
- any Phase 6C prediction quantity to which the record may later be compared;
- the permitted Phase 6E comparison policy.

If Phase 6D finds a genuine contradiction in the frozen status, it must report
that contradiction explicitly and stop Phase 6E until a separately documented
registry amendment is made.

## No numerical goodness-of-fit in Phase 6D

Phase 6D must not calculate or report:

- signed residual;
- absolute residual;
- relative residual;
- percent residual;
- ratio of prediction to historical value;
- target ranking;
- pass/fail against a tolerance;
- significance;
- "success" or "failure" of a prediction.

The Phase 6D implementation may verify the identity and hash of the Phase 6C
artifact and inspect the names of available prediction quantities.

It must not use the numerical `value` fields in
`dimensional_predictions` to determine or justify a status.

## Status cannot be upgraded because of numerical agreement

A quantity that was available during model development remains a:

```text
development_used_target
```

even if Phase 6E later finds extremely small numerical residuals.

Likewise:

- a `construction_input` remains an input;
- a `unit_definition` remains a definition;
- a `construction_identity` remains an identity;
- a `descriptive_correspondence` does not become a prediction;
- a `not_evaluable_from_v0_5` record cannot be made evaluable by extending or
  refitting the frozen v0.5 geometry.

No downstream result may promote one of these records to
`frozen_unused_target`.

No historical commit is rewritten.

## Equivalent historical expressions and double counting

Where one registry record contains equivalent source expressions in two units,
Phase 6E must not count them as two independent comparisons.

Example:

```text
WALL12-001:
    3264 current feet
    1200 Megalithic Yards
```

Under the registered source unit convention these are the same dimensional
statement.

The canonical residual for a record uses the registry's primary
`source_value` / `source_unit`.

A secondary value may be checked for conversion consistency, but it does not
create another target or another degree of evidential support.

## Duplicate area statements

`WALL12-003` and `SOMM-003` both state approximately the same wall area but
belong to distinct source passages / attribution contexts.

Phase 6E may report both rows because both are historically registered, but it
must explicitly flag that they are not independent numerical evidence.

They may not be counted as two independent successes.

## Comparison-policy definitions

### `no_residual`

The number is an input, definition, or source arithmetic identity for which a
prediction residual would be evidentially misleading.

### `retrospective_residual`

A residual may be calculated because it is useful for quantifying how closely
the frozen geometry reproduces a historical target.

Interpretation must explicitly state that the target was available during
development/model selection.

### `descriptive_residual`

A numerical difference may be shown, but the historical quantity is not an
independent target and no predictive interpretation is attached.

### `context_only`

The record may be displayed as historical context but receives no residual and
no pass/fail decision.

### `not_evaluable`

The required geometric object is absent from v0.5. Phase 6E must preserve the
record as unevaluated rather than extend the model.
