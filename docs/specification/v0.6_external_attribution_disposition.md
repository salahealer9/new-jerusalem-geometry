# v0.6 Phase 6F — external-attribution disposition

## Status

Deferred.

No external-attribution source was added to the v0.6 evidential baseline.

## Purpose

Phase 6F was reserved for optional independent verification of external
attributions appearing in John Michell's metrological discussion.

The numerical audit through Phase 6E does not require this expansion.

Accordingly, v0.6 closes the optional branch by recording what is and is not
verified rather than silently enlarging the source corpus after the numerical
results are known.

## Baseline source boundary

The v0.6 historical-metrology baseline remains restricted to the two Michell
primary texts already used by the project:

- John Michell, *City of Revelation*;
- John Michell with Allan Brown, *How the World Is Made*.

The project verifies what Michell states, adopts, or reports in those texts.

It does not claim, within v0.6, that every external attribution made by Michell
has independently been checked against the external author's original work.

## Frozen provenance taxonomy

The Phase 6A attribution taxonomy remains unchanged:

```text
michell_stated
michell_adopts_external
michell_reports_other
project_derived
```

Phase 6F does not promote any row from:

```text
michell_adopts_external
michell_reports_other
```

to an independently externally verified status.

No Phase 6A row is edited.

## External attributions intentionally not independently verified in v0.6

### Alexander Thom

Michell attributes the Megalithic Yard of approximately 2.72 current feet to
Alexander Thom.

For v0.6:

```text
verified:
    Michell reports/adopts this attribution and unit value.

not independently verified:
    Thom's original publication record and exact wording.
```

The Phase 6B value remains a source-defined unit convention for purposes of
the Michell audit. Its use does not imply independent endorsement of Thom's
historical or archaeological claims.

### A. E. Berriman

Michell attributes or develops metrological relations involving the Sumerian
foot and related ancient units with reference to Berriman.

For v0.6:

```text
verified:
    Michell gives the registered values and attribution.

not independently verified:
    Berriman's original source, derivation, terminology, and historical claim.
```

The frozen Phase 6B conversions remain tests of Michell's stated/adopted
metrological system, not independent confirmation of Berriman.

### Ian Sommerville

Michell presents the later New Jerusalem wall dimensional analysis as work by
Ian Sommerville and cites a 1974 paper.

For v0.6:

```text
verified:
    Michell reports the registered wall dimensions, working tolerance,
    old-English-foot relation, and attribution to Sommerville.

not independently verified:
    the cited 1974 paper against an independently obtained original copy.
```

This does not alter the Phase 6D leakage result.

The Sommerville dimensional quantities remain:

```text
development_used_target
```

where frozen as such, because the repository history demonstrates that those
values were available during model development.

Independent retrieval of Sommerville's original paper would therefore improve
historical attribution confidence but would not convert the Phase 6E
retrospective residuals into independent forward predictions.

## Why external verification is deferred

Independent external-source verification would require a new source corpus and
a separate provenance exercise after the Phase 6E numerical results are already
known.

Adding those sources now is unnecessary for the stated v0.6 question:

> Given the geometry frozen at v0.5.0, which dimensional and metrological
> quantities in Michell's primary texts are inputs, identities,
> development-used targets, unused targets, or descriptive correspondences,
> and what residuals follow under the source-stated units without refitting?

That question has been answered without external-attribution expansion.

## Effect on the Phase 6E numerical results

None.

Phase 6F:

- changes no historical target;
- changes no unit definition;
- changes no prediction;
- changes no comparison policy;
- changes no residual;
- introduces no tolerance;
- introduces no new claim of independence;
- introduces no new model parameter.

The Phase 6E artifact hashes remain the canonical numerical result.

## Future verification rule

External attribution verification may be undertaken later as a dedicated
historical-source audit.

Such work must:

1. identify the independently obtained external source;
2. record edition/publication metadata;
3. quote or transcribe only the necessary source statement;
4. distinguish agreement with Michell's report from disagreement with it;
5. avoid altering the frozen v0.6 numerical artifacts;
6. document any proposed registry amendment explicitly and prospectively;
7. never retroactively upgrade a development-used target into an independent
   prediction.

## Phase 6F conclusion

The external-attribution branch is closed for v0.6 as:

```text
DEFERRED — NOT REQUIRED FOR NUMERICAL BASELINE
```

This is a scope decision, not a negative finding about Thom, Berriman, or
Sommerville.

The project may now proceed to Phase 6G checkpoint closeout.
