## Residual formulas

For a predicted quantity `P` and historical target `T`:

```text
signed_residual = P - T

absolute_residual = abs(P - T)

relative_residual = (P - T) / T

percent_residual = 100 * (P - T) / T

absolute_percent_residual = abs(percent_residual)
```

Positive signed residual means the frozen prediction is larger than the
historical value.

The same formulas apply to dimensionless ratio comparison.

## Numerical arithmetic

Historical targets and dimensional comparison arithmetic should use a
deterministic high-precision decimal representation where practical.

The frozen Phase 7B floating-point geometry must not be recomputed at higher
precision in Phase 7D merely to improve historical agreement.

The audit evaluates the values that were actually frozen in Phase 7B.

## Forbidden operations

After historical target loading, Phase 7D and its implementation must not:

- modify the polar-pivot wall;
- modify Phase 7B vertex coordinates;
- modify semantic vertex classes;
- modify the regular baseline;
- alter the 720 current-foot scale;
- choose a different unit convention to reduce residuals;
- optimize any geometric parameter;
- switch construction candidates;
- alter correspondence between radius classes and historical values;
- introduce a post-hoc acceptance tolerance;
- delete an unfavorable registered comparison;
- round predictions to historical integers before residual calculation.

## Figure 30 tolerance

Figure 30 also reports Sommerville working to a tolerance of 1:2500 in the
dodecagon discussion.

That historical tolerance is context only.

It is not a preregistered v0.7 acceptance threshold and must not be used to
classify the radius comparisons as pass/fail.

A later report may state the numerical scale of the residuals alongside the
historical tolerance descriptively, provided it is clearly not treated as a
prospective threshold.
