# Geometry Specification

Version: 0.1.0-draft

## Status

This document is a working mathematical specification. Definitions may change
after comparison with the primary sources.

## Normalisation

Let `u > 0` be the fundamental unit of length and let the common centre be

    O = (0, 0).

The construction is scale invariant.

## Core dimensions

- Earth diameter: `11u`
- Earth radius: `11u / 2`
- Moon diameter: `3u`
- Moon radius: `3u / 2`
- Construction-circle radius: `7u`
- Earth-square side: `11u`

## Core identity

    7u = 11u / 2 + 3u / 2

Therefore a Moon circle whose centre lies on the radius-7 construction circle
and whose radius is `3u / 2` can be externally tangent to the Earth circle.

## Perimeter approximation

The Earth-square perimeter is

    4 × 11u = 44u.

The radius-7 circle has circumference

    14πu,

which equals `44u` only under the approximation `π = 22/7`.

The relation is therefore approximate in Euclidean geometry.
