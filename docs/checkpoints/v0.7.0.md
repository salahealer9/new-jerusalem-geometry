# v0.7.0 — Sommerville dodecagon vertex-radius audit

Release date: 2026-08-08

## Scope

v0.7.0 resolves the Figure 30 radius problem associated with Ian Sommerville's
irregular New Jerusalem dodecagon without introducing a new geometric fit.

The work began from the literal Figure 30 prose term `decagon`. Source
inspection then showed that the numerical radii belong to the already-present
irregular outer dodecagon. The project preserves the literal source wording in
the frozen v0.6 registry while recording the corrected geometric referent
prospectively in v0.7.

## Analytical chain

```text
Phase 7A  source clarification:
          literal "decagon" wording -> geometric dodecagon referent

Phase 7B  frozen vertex-radius audit:
          semantic 8+4 radius classes tested without historical targets

Phase 7C  geometry freeze:
          existing v0.5 POLAR_PIVOT_WALL accepted as the complete
          relevant dodecagon geometry; no new construction introduced

Phase 7D  dimensional / historical audit:
          frozen radius classes transformed by the frozen 720 ft/u scale
          and compared with Figure 30 source quantities
```

## Main geometric result

The preferred reconstruction is not a new polygon:

```text
SOMMERVILLE_DODECAGON_RECONSTRUCTION
    =
frozen v0.5 POLAR_PIVOT_WALL
```

Phase 7B classified vertices semantically from incident side classes before
examining radius values.

```text
POLAR_ADJACENT_VERTEX
    count = 8
    mean normalized radius = 8.79984753348571 u

OBLIQUE_PAIR_VERTEX
    count = 4
    mean normalized radius = 8.75294593972281 u
```

The eight polar-adjacent vertices lie on the parameter-free regular-dodecagon
baseline radius:

```text
regular baseline radius = 8.79984753348571 u
polar minus regular      = 0 u
```

The four oblique-pair vertices are displaced inward:

```text
oblique minus regular = -0.0469015937628932 u
```

The resulting normalized radius ratio is:

```text
1.00535837809189
```

No radius clustering, historical target, dimensional scale, or new geometric
parameter was used to obtain the two classes.

## Dimensional result

The already-frozen v0.6 scale is:

```text
1 normalized unit = 720 current feet
```

Applying that scale after the geometry freeze gives:

```text
long / polar-adjacent radius
    6335.8902241097076 current ft

short / oblique-pair radius
    6302.12107660042464 current ft
```

Figure 30 reports the corresponding historical values as:

```text
6336 current ft
6300 current ft
176:175
6336 = 31680 / 5
```

## Evidential dependence

The four rows are not four independent numerical matches.

```text
Primary historical dimensional observations:
    D7-001  6336 ft
    D7-002  6300 ft

Derived / equivalent relations:
    D7-003  176/175, derived from the long/short pair
    D7-004  31680/5, numerically equivalent to D7-001
```

The Figure 30 source had been consulted during earlier wall-model development.
Accordingly, these comparisons are not described as blind or statistically
independent predictions.

The narrower no-refit result is:

> The v0.5 wall geometry and the v0.7 two-radius structure were frozen without
> fitting the 6336, 6300, or 176:175 quantities, and the frozen reconstruction
> subsequently reproduces those source values closely under the already-frozen
> scale.

## Historical tolerance boundary

Figure 30 reports Sommerville working to a tolerance of `1:2500` in the
surrounding dodecagon discussion.

v0.7 records that statement as historical context only. It is not used as a
prospective or retrospective pass/fail threshold.

## Original v0.7 preregistration disposition

The initial v0.7 preregistration treated the source word `decagon` literally
and proposed reconstruction of a separate object.

That path was not silently rewritten.

Phase 7A records the source correction, and the later preregistered phases
operate on the corrected geometric referent:

```text
literal source term:       decagon
operational referent:      dodecagon_vertex_radii
frozen geometry required:  existing 12-sided POLAR_PIVOT_WALL
new polygon required:      no
```

This is a documented change in source interpretation, not a target-driven
change in geometry.
