# v0.6.0 — Historical metrology and prediction-status audit

Release date: 2026-08-08

## Scope

v0.6.0 audits the dimensional and metrological claims surrounding the frozen
v0.5.0 `NJG_MICHELL` reconstruction. The geometry itself is not refitted.

The audit separates construction inputs, unit definitions, construction
identities, development-used targets, descriptive correspondences, and claims
that are not evaluable from the frozen v0.5 geometry before calculating any
historical residual.

## Frozen analytical chain

```text
Phase 6A  historical metrology registry
Phase 6B  formal source-defined unit system
Phase 6C  frozen dimensional predictions
Phase 6D  prediction-status / leakage audit
Phase 6E  historical residual + sensitivity audit
Phase 6F  external-attribution disposition
```

The baseline source corpus remains John Michell's *City of Revelation* and John
Michell with Allan Brown's *How the World Is Made*.

External verification of Thom, Berriman, and Sommerville is explicitly deferred
and is not required for the v0.6 numerical baseline.

## Interpretation

The later Sommerville dimensional layer is reproduced particularly closely:

```text
polar side residual          -0.00920%
oblique side residual        +0.03427%
wall area residual           +0.002166%
old-English perimeter        +0.03827%
```

These are very close retrospective agreements, not blind or statistically
independent predictions, because the historical dimensions were available
during wall-model development and assessment.

The earlier Figure 12 mean-side/perimeter layer differs by approximately
+0.305754%, while the later Sommerville side/perimeter/area layer lies on a
sub-0.04% scale.

No aggregate goodness-of-fit statistic, ranking, combined score, p-value,
likelihood, Bayes factor, success count, or post-hoc threshold is calculated.

## Sensitivity boundary

All seven unit-reexpression checks pass. No source uncertainty is inferred from
qualitative wording such as "close", "approximately", or "virtually".

The historically reported `1:2500` working tolerance remains context only and
is not used as an acceptance threshold.

## Unevaluated claims

The registered Sommerville decagon quantities remain unevaluable from frozen
v0.5 geometry. v0.6 does not extend the model simply to obtain another match.
