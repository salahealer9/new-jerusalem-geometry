## Scope

Version `v0.3.0` completes the direct source-plate calibration of John
Michell's Figure 12 Moon-circle and outer-wall geometry.

The checkpoint adds:

* a fixed Figure 12 source-preparation and provenance protocol;
* three independent 120-observation digitisation passes;
* quality-control analysis of registration landmarks, Moon circles, and wall
  lines;
* similarity, affine, and projective registration comparison;
* selection of the centroid affine registration;
* source-derived normalized Moon-circle geometry;
* source-derived normalized twelve-sided wall geometry;
* fixed-candidate comparison of four Moon-placement models;
* source-calibrated comparison of competing wall rules;
* exact polar-pivot tangent wall construction;
* three-model complete exact-wall comparison;
* independent comparison with Michell's later Sommerville dimensions;
* deterministic Figure 12 wall-evidence SVG and JSON;
* preservation of the earlier radial-support construction as a historical
  project inference;
* a geometric claim matrix with unique claim identifiers;
* updated source-audit and public-facing documentation.

## Source registration result

The selected centroid affine registration gives approximately:

* training RMS: 1.342228 pixels;
* leave-one-out RMS: 1.790924 pixels;
* maximum leave-one-out residual: 2.541469 pixels;
* anisotropy ratio: 1.013122338.

The project therefore uses the affine registration for Figure 12
source-derived normalized geometry.

## Source-derived Figure 12 geometry

The affine-calibrated source plate gives approximately:

* mean Moon-centre radius from the origin: 7.015679573 normalized units;
* mean fitted Moon radius: 1.520234700 normalized units;
* wall perimeter: 54.773997038 normalized units;
* wall area: 233.140234440 normalized units squared.

The plate is treated as a measured printed source, not as an exact Euclidean
construction.

## Moon-placement comparison

The source-derived oblique Moon centres give the fixed-candidate RMS ordering:

```text
NJG_SVG         0.029832828 u
NJG_INC         0.030299159 u
NJG_28          0.034811987 u
NJG_MICHELL_28  0.044859702 u
```

All three independent digitisation passes reproduce this ordering.

The observed angular scatter of the printed plate is larger than the very
small separation between the leading candidates. The plate preference for
`NJG_SVG` is therefore recorded as reproducible but not as a unique
identification.

Michell's textual square-circle incidence constraint remains represented by
`NJG_INC`.

## Outer-wall reconstruction

Three complete exact `NJG_INC` wall candidates were compared against the same
affine-calibrated Figure 12 wall.

| Wall model                  |           Angle RMS |   Support RMS |
| --------------------------- | ------------------: | ------------: |
| `POLAR_PIVOT_TANGENT`       | 1.019835793 degrees | 0.038999406 u |
| `REGULAR_DIRECTION_TANGENT` | 1.458881984 degrees | 0.036819219 u |
| `RADIAL_SUPPORT`            | 4.755395950 degrees | 0.032822024 u |

The angular ranking is reproduced in all three independent digitisation
passes:

```text
POLAR_PIVOT_TANGENT
    < REGULAR_DIRECTION_TANGENT
    << RADIAL_SUPPORT
```

The support-distance metric gives the opposite preference at its leading end:
`RADIAL_SUPPORT` has the smallest support RMS.

This counter-evidence is retained explicitly.

## Polar-pivot construction

The preferred combined reconstruction begins from a regular dodecagon of
apothem 8.5 normalized units.

The four polar sides remain fixed. Each of the eight oblique sides retains its
endpoint on the neighbouring polar side as a pivot and rotates inward until it
is tangent to its corresponding `NJG_INC` Moon circle.

For exact `NJG_INC` geometry this gives:

* four polar sides:
  4.555136271329 u = 3279.698115 ft;
* eight oblique sides:
  4.543223126508 u = 3271.120651 ft;
* perimeter:
  54.566330097384 u;
* area:
  231.486496089401 u^2 =
  120,002,599.573 ft^2;
* old-English-foot perimeter:
  approximately 36,013.778 ft.

## Historical dimensional comparison

Michell's later account of Ian Sommerville's analysis gives approximately:

* four longer polar sides: 3280 ft;
* eight shorter sides: 3270 ft;
* area: 120,000,000 square ft;
* perimeter: virtually 36,000 old English ft;
* working tolerance: 1:2500.

The polar-pivot reconstruction differs by approximately:

* polar side: -0.009204 percent;
* oblique side: +0.034271 percent;
* area: +0.002166 percent;
* old-English perimeter: +0.038272 percent.

All four numerical differences are therefore at or below approximately
0.04 percent.

This independent dimensional closure is a major reason that
`POLAR_PIVOT_TANGENT` is preferred over the earlier radial-support inference.

## Evidence status

The present evidence supports the following hierarchy:

`RADIAL_SUPPORT`
: historical project inference retained for reproducibility.

`REGULAR_DIRECTION_TANGENT`
: useful intermediate wall-rule comparison.

`POLAR_PIVOT_TANGENT`
: current preferred source-supported project reconstruction.

The pivot operation itself is not claimed to be an explicitly stated or
uniquely established historical construction algorithm.

## Relationship to earlier checkpoints

The signed `v0.1.0` checkpoint preserves the initial architectural
reconstruction.

The signed `v0.2.0` checkpoint preserves the direct Figure 14
source-calibration result and the correction of the canonical printed
heptagram topology to `{7/2}`.

Version `v0.3.0` adds the corresponding direct source-calibration layer for
Figure 12 and supersedes the earlier radial-support wall as the preferred
outer-wall interpretation.

The radial-support implementation and its historical generated figure remain
preserved rather than being rewritten or deleted.

## Interpretation boundary

This checkpoint supports a reproducible geometric reconstruction and
source-plate audit of Figure 12.

It does not establish that Michell's printed plate is an exact Euclidean
construction.

It does not establish that the polar-pivot algorithm was explicitly used by
Michell or Sommerville.

It does not use symbolic, numerological, gematria, or other interpretive
claims as evidence for the geometric reconstruction.
