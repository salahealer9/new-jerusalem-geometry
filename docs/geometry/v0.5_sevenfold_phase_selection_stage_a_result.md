## Scientific interpretation

This is a substantive result rather than a failure to obtain a result.

The 28-point construction generates a four-member family of symmetry-related
seven-point candidates.

The construction grammar determines the family but, at Stage A, does not
determine its orientation.

Consequently the orientation used by the current Figure 14 reconstruction is
an additional discrete choice.

That remaining choice must not be concealed by treating the current
implementation as if it had been uniquely generated by the grammar.
