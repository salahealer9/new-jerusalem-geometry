# v0.8 Figure 194 dual-method synthesis

## Scope

Version `v0.8.0` audits the two approximate sevenfold constructions presented
by John Michell in Figure 194 of *How the World Is Made*.

The source describes two distinct methods and explicitly states that exact
sevenfold division is impossible by these elementary constructions. It says
that both are close approximations and specifically states that the first
method is accurate to better than one part in one thousand.

The two source branches are preserved as distinct constructions:

```text
Method 1
7 -> 14 -> 28

Method 2
7 -> 21 -> 42
```

No combined, averaged, or optimized construction is attributed to Michell.

```

```

## Local dual-method comparison

The exact regular sevenfold reference is:

```text
alpha_exact = 2*pi/7
            = 51.42857142857143 degrees
```

The frozen local comparison gives:

| Quantity | Method 1 | Method 2 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| step angle | 51.47070143243995 deg | 51.31781254651057 deg |
| signed error | +0.042130003868525 deg | -0.110758882060864 deg |
| absolute percent error | 0.081919451966577% | 0.215364492896125% |
| seven-step closure | +0.294910027079657 deg | -0.775312174426036 deg |

Therefore:

```text
alpha_2 < alpha_exact < alpha_1
```

and the two local errors have opposite signs.

Method 1 is the more accurate of the two local sevenfold constructions. The
absolute Method 2 local error is approximately:

```text
2.628978682425679
```

times the Method 1 local error.

The arithmetic midpoint of the two angles happens to lie closer to exact
sevenfold division than either Method 2 or the Method 1 error magnitude, but
that midpoint is retained only as an explicitly exploratory arithmetic
quantity. It is not a Michell construction and is not evidence that the two
methods were intended to compensate.

## Method 2 local construction

For a construction-circle radius `R`, the inscribed equilateral-triangle side
is:

```text
sqrt(3)*R
```

The Figure 194 midpoint arc has radius:

```text
sqrt(3)*R/2
```

and its intersection chord with the construction circle therefore gives:

```text
cos(alpha_2) = 5/8
alpha_2 = acos(5/8)
```

The implementation identity is satisfied to floating-point precision.

To turn the source-described local step into a deterministic complete
seven-mark set, the project preregistered the operational completion:

```text
theta(gamma, k)
    = normalize(gamma + k*alpha_2)

k = -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3
```

Here `k = 0` is the carrier triangle vertex and `k = +/-1` are the two
source-described arc-circle intersections.

The repeated-step completion for `k = +/-2, +/-3` is explicitly a project
operationalization of the source phrase "seven virtually equal parts"; it is
not represented as a further formula stated by Michell.

## Exact symbolic explanation

Define the positive Method 2 local deficit:

```text
delta = 2*pi/7 - alpha_2
```

Then the complete propagated nonuniformity follows exactly from that one local
quantity:

```text
RMS_21   = sqrt(10)*delta
MAX_21   = 5*delta
RANGE_21 = 7*delta

RMS_42   = sqrt(6)*delta
MAX_42   = 6*delta
RANGE_42 = 7*delta
```

Consequently:

```text
RMS_42 / RMS_21
    = sqrt(3/5)

MAX_42 / MAX_21
    = 6/5

RANGE_42 / RANGE_21
    = 1

normalized_RMS_42 / normalized_RMS_21
    = 2*sqrt(3/5)
```

The common gap range is also exactly the magnitude of the local seven-step
closure defect:

```text
RANGE_21
= RANGE_42
= 7*delta
= abs(7*alpha_2 - 2*pi)
```

The symbolic identities were recognized after the Phase 8E numerical result.
Their Phase 8F verification is therefore a **post-result explanatory
derivation**, not preregistered confirmation.

## What the reciprocal stage changes

The 42-point reciprocal-triangle stage cannot be described by one unqualified
statement such as "more regular" or "less regular".

In absolute angular units:

```text
RMS residual decreases:
sqrt(10)*delta -> sqrt(6)*delta
```

but:

```text
worst individual residual increases:
5*delta -> 6*delta
```

and because the regular reference gap halves:

```text
normalized RMS residual increases
by 2*sqrt(3/5).
```

The conclusion therefore depends on the registered metric.

## Interpretation boundary

The v0.8 result establishes a reproducible mathematical reconstruction of the
two Figure 194 approximate construction families under the project's explicit
operational rules.

It does not establish:

- that either approximation is an exact sevenfold construction;
- that Michell intended the two local errors to compensate;
- that Michell intended an arithmetic average of the methods;
- that the symbolic gap identities were historically known or intended;
- that Method 2 should replace the existing Method 1 composite;
- physical, archaeological, astronomical, or statistical significance.

A direct Method 1 28-point versus Method 2 42-point ranking remains deferred
because the source describes distinct construction branches with different
cardinalities and does not provide a common ranking criterion.
