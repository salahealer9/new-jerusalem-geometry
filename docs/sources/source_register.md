The Wikimedia SVG is treated as a historical computational reconstruction, not
as definitive evidence of Michell's original construction.

## Source policy

Every geometric claim should be labelled as one of:

- directly stated in a primary source;
- derived mathematically from stated dimensions;
- implemented in the Wikimedia reconstruction;
- proposed in the present project;
- approximate or interpretive.

Page numbers, figure numbers, editions, and quotations will be added as the
source audit proceeds.
