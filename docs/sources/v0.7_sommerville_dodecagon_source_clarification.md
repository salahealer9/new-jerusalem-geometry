# v0.7 — Sommerville dodecagon source clarification

## Status

Prospective Phase 7A source correction recorded after the v0.7 preregistration
and before implementation of any new v0.7 geometry.

The original preregistration remains immutable.

## Source observation

In *How the World Is Made*, Figure 30 concerns the twelve-sided figure drawn
outside the twelve lunar circles.

The accompanying text first identifies this figure as a dodecagon and discusses
its four polar sides and eight shorter non-polar sides.

Later in the same paragraph the source refers to:

```text
the two radii to the decagon, 6336 and 6300
````

The paragraph then cites Ian Sommerville's 1974 paper:

```text
A Note on the Dimensions of the Irregular Dodecagon
Associated with the New Jerusalem
```

Inspection of Figure 30 shows the quantities 6336 and 6300 on radial segments
from the common centre to vertices of the same outer twelve-sided wall.

No separate ten-sided polygon is identified in Figure 30.

## Operational interpretation

For v0.7, the most conservative geometric interpretation is therefore:

```text
"decagon" in the Figure 30 prose is a wording/editorial anomaly;
6336 and 6300 are radii to vertices of the irregular dodecagon.
```

The project does not silently alter the source wording.

It records both:

```text
literal_source_term = decagon
geometric_source_interpretation = dodecagon_vertex_radii
```

## Consequence for the v0.6 registry

The frozen v0.6 registry classified records DECAGON-001 through DECAGON-004 as
`not_evaluable_from_v0_5` because they were interpreted as referring to a
separate decagon object absent from the v0.5 composite.

That classification remains historically frozen in v0.6.

Phase 7A corrects the interpretation prospectively:

```text
DECAGON-001 -> dodecagon long vertex-radius claim
DECAGON-002 -> dodecagon short vertex-radius claim
DECAGON-003 -> ratio of the two dodecagon vertex-radius classes
DECAGON-004 -> long dodecagon vertex radius relative to 31680
```

The record IDs are retained when referring to the historical v0.6 registry.

No v0.6 artifact is modified.

## Existing geometric availability

The frozen v0.5 `POLAR_PIVOT_WALL` already contains the twelve dodecagon
vertices.

Therefore no new polygon needs to be invented in order to evaluate the two
radius classes.

Phase 7B must first audit those existing frozen vertices.

## Evidential boundary

The historical values

```text
6336
6300
176:175
1/5 of 31680
```

remain exposed historical quantities.

Their newly recognized evaluability does not make them independent blind
predictions.

They must be treated as retrospective source-consistency quantities.

## Effect on the v0.7 SVG plan

The preregistered visual roles are retained but their geometric subject is
corrected from a separate decagon to the existing outer dodecagon.

The intended sequence becomes:

```text
sommerville_dodecagon_source_constraints.svg
sommerville_dodecagon_regular_baseline.svg
sommerville_dodecagon_reconstruction.svg
```

The first four remain geometry-first artifacts.

Historical dimensional target values appear only in the downstream dimensional
audit SVG.

## Phase 7A conclusion

The v0.7 problem is no longer:

```text
construct an unresolved Sommerville decagon
```

but:

```text
audit the two vertex-radius classes of the already reconstructed
irregular Sommerville/New-Jerusalem dodecagon.
```

This correction was established from source interpretation before new v0.7
geometry implementation.
