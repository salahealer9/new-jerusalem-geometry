## Primary source

John Michell with Allan Brown, *How the World Is Made*, Figure 194,
printed page 221 / PDF page 239.

Figure 194 states that exact septenary division is unavailable in the shown
construction and presents two close approximations.

The already implemented Method 1 follows the source chain:

```text
7 -> 14 -> 28
```

The newly registered Method 2 follows the distinct source chain:

```text
7 -> 21 -> 42
```

The source explicitly states the better-than-one-part-in-one-thousand accuracy
for Method 1. Phase 8A does not transfer that quantitative statement to Method
2.

## Method 2 source construction

The source-described Method 2 begins with an equilateral triangle inscribed in
the circle.

From one triangle vertex an arc is drawn so that it bisects the two incident
triangle sides. The two intersections of that arc with the circumcircle are
described as seven-division points.

Two corresponding arcs from the other two triangle vertices extend the
construction to twenty-one parts.

A reciprocal triangle is then added, producing a hexagon within the circle and
a forty-two-point division.

## Project operational reading

For the local Method 2 step, let:

- `O` be the circumcentre;
- `R` be the circumradius;
- `A` be the selected equilateral-triangle vertex;
- `M` and `N` be the midpoints of the two triangle sides incident at `A`;
- `P` be either intersection of the `A`-centred arc with the circumcircle.

The equilateral triangle has side length

```text
s = sqrt(3) R
```

and the arc radius is therefore

```text
AM = AN = s/2 = sqrt(3) R / 2.
```

Since `OA = OP = R`, the chord relation for the central angle `alpha_2`
between `A` and `P` gives

```text
AP^2 = 2 R^2 (1 - cos(alpha_2)).
```

Substituting

```text
AP = sqrt(3) R / 2
```

gives

```text
3 R^2 / 4 = 2 R^2 (1 - cos(alpha_2))
```

and hence

```text
cos(alpha_2) = 5/8
alpha_2 = arccos(5/8).
```

This is a project derivation from the textual construction, not a fitted
quantity and not a claim that Michell states the analytic formula.
